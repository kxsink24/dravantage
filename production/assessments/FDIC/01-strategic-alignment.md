## Mission and Mandate

Old Dominion University is a public research university in Norfolk, VA (~23,500 students), pursuing
**Carnegie R1** research status while serving a high proportion of online and low-income students. [[gemini-deep-research]]

Active mandates and commitments that govern how it operates:
- **SACSCOC accreditation** — reaffirmed 2023; the QEP ("PURSUE TRUTH") imposes ongoing data-collection and institutional-effectiveness reporting obligations. [[gemini-deep-research]]
- **SCHEV oversight** — Virginia's outcomes-based funding plan ("Developing Tomorrow's Talent," through 2032) ties state funding to retention, time-to-degree, and post-graduation outcomes. [[gemini-deep-research]]
- **EVMS integration mandate (HB1400)** — the July 2024 merger creating Macon & Joan Brock Virginia Health Sciences carries state reporting requirements and large funding commitments. [[gemini-deep-research]]
- **OMNI "Salesforce First"** — a President's-Cabinet-sponsored mandate to prioritize Salesforce-integrating systems in all procurements. [[gemini-deep-research]]

## Strategic Priorities (12–24 month horizon)

- **"One Monarch Nation" (OMNI)** — a unified, real-time, 360° student experience on a Salesforce-first foundation. [[gemini-deep-research]] [[2026-05-11-kickoff]]
- **Grow online & global enrollment** to offset a decade-long ~30% on-campus undergraduate decline; international enrollment fell 11.6% (2024→2025, ~$2.4M tuition loss). [[gemini-deep-research]]
- **Lift retention toward 82% and six-year graduation toward 57% by 2028** (published strategic-plan targets). [[gemini-deep-research]]
- **Integrate the EVMS / health-sciences programs** (+1,440 students, +2,195 employees) into core administrative and CRM systems. [[gemini-deep-research]]
- **Resolve the MuleSoft vs. Informatica direction** and a broader fall integrations-modernization effort. [[slack-history]] [[2026-05-11-kickoff]]

## Compelling Event

ODU has committed to a "Salesforce First" foundation for One Monarch Nation, but the authoritative
student data still lives in **Banner and moves in batches** — and the EVMS merger plus SCHEV
outcomes-funding are raising the cost of that gap now. (Matches `DRA_Engagement__c.Compelling_Event__c`.) [[gemini-deep-research]] [[2026-05-11-kickoff]]
