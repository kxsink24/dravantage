## Decision and narrative direction

- The team chose a **path-of-least-resistance** approach for the three-minute governor/Mark video: show immediate, achievable value without asking the state to re-architect its entire healthcare data environment. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- Grants management should receive a brief, high-level treatment; the main story should focus on what can be done with available data and how that supports rural-health outcomes. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- The message should be citizen- and outcome-centered rather than a feature/function or infrastructure demonstration. Proposed examples included rural health, Native Hawaiian communities, homelessness, and hotspot visibility. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- The team wants a high-level, technology-agnostic integrated-health-data-platform visual, followed by deeper engagement once the right accountable stakeholders and champions are identified. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]

## Stakeholder perspectives and tensions

- **Governor-facing vision:** The discussion referenced a desire for better visibility into rural-health needs and a future in which citizens can access their data more directly. The transcript also referenced rural health, Native Hawaiian communities, and homelessness as likely executive story anchors. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **RHTP Oversight Committee:** The team characterized this group as focused on data governance and the question of who should own or be accountable for the data. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **SHPDA / Jack:** The team characterized Jack at SHPDA as focused on smart-network outcomes and administrative cost savings, with reluctance to own PII or become a technology manager. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Provider-level delivery:** The discussion recognized that the state is not the direct clinical-care provider and that proactive clinical outreach may belong with hospitals, clinics, or other provider organizations rather than state staff. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Internal team:** The team repeatedly identified a need to narrow the story, identify the accountable buyer/sponsor, and avoid presenting an expansive "we can do everything" platform message. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]

## 3 Deep Dive Rule — evidence chains

### 1. Unclear ownership and direction

- **Symptom:** The team described multiple competing RHTP perspectives and uncertainty about whether the state should own the data, provide analytics, or leave clinical action to providers. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Operational consequence:** The internal team cannot confidently choose the audience, use case, or accountable sponsor for the three-minute video and deeper DRA conversations. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Business impact:** The initiative risks continued analysis paralysis and delayed movement despite executive interest and upcoming funding/procurement timing. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Capability hypotheses:** Understand, Protect, Decide, Orchestrate.

### 2. Governance and liability concerns

- **Symptom:** Participants described state-level reluctance to handle clinical data, PII, or direct clinical-care responsibility. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Operational consequence:** Potential use cases are narrowed or reframed before technical feasibility is even assessed; proposed golden-record and platform conversations must address privacy, security, access, and accountability. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Business impact:** A technically compelling solution may fail to gain sponsorship if the state believes it is accepting unowned clinical-data liability. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Capability hypotheses:** Protect, Unify, Understand.

### 3. Broad messaging has not created movement

- **Symptom:** The team stated that it has presented broad capabilities for roughly a year without gaining traction and described the current state as analysis paralysis. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Operational consequence:** The team is being asked to compress a large transformation into a focused three-minute narrative and select one achievable starting point. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Business impact:** Delay threatens the ability to influence Year 2 funding/RFP discussions expected in October and reduces confidence that the current approach will produce an actionable next step. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **Capability hypotheses:** Decide, Act, Orchestrate.
