# Strategic Alignment

**Maps to:** DRA Report Section 1 — Vision & Case for Change

> [!WARNING]

## Mission and Mandate

The public-research briefing frames Hawaii's health transformation around universal access, rural-health capacity, health equity, interoperability, workforce, and readiness for value-based payment. SHPDA is presented as a health-planning and coordination authority; Med-QUEST's Health Analytics Program as the operational steward for claims/APCD work; HHIE and University of Hawaii as ecosystem and data partners. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]

Relevant mandates and commitments to validate:

- Rural Health Transformation Program funding and its associated reporting, sub-award, provider-readiness, and infrastructure requirements. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
- Preparation for CMS AHEAD-style global budgets and advanced primary-care/value-based payment readiness. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
- Statewide health-data exchange, claims analytics, privacy, security, and accountable cross-agency governance. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
- Public-sector health equity priorities involving rural communities, Native Hawaiian populations, people experiencing homelessness, and underserved neighbor-island populations. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]] [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]

**Confidence:** Medium for public mandate signals; Low for current decision rights and operating ownership.

## Strategic Priorities (12–24 month horizon)

1. **Stand up RHTP programs and sub-awards.** The research briefing identifies RHIN, RVBI, telehealth, emergency access, workforce, and rural-respite initiatives that require program, provider, and compliance coordination. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
2. **Prepare rural providers for value-based payment and AHEAD-related risk.** The RVBI/Virtual CIN/MSO hypothesis centers on provider onboarding, coaching, analytics, quality reporting, and readiness tracking. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
3. **Improve health-data exchange and analytics without forcing premature state ownership of clinical data.** The internal team explicitly chose a path-of-least-resistance message and warned against a top-down rearchitecture pitch. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
4. **Reduce administrative burden and improve visibility into rural-health outcomes.** The internal discussion favored an outcome story around rural health, Native Hawaiian communities, homelessness, and hotspot visibility rather than feature demonstrations. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
5. **Create accountable governance across SHPDA, DOH, Med-QUEST, HHIE, ETS, providers, and community organizations.** Both inputs identify ownership, decision rights, privacy, and cross-vendor coordination as unresolved constraints. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]] [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]

## Compelling Event

RHTP planning, sub-award, compliance, and provider-readiness work is occurring ahead of major funding and value-based-care milestones, while the internal team is trying to narrow a broad transformation story into an actionable first step within a short executive-video window. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]] [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]

## Magic Wand Responses / Desired Outcomes

These are hypotheses inferred from internal discussion and public research, not direct customer answers:

- **Executive / Governor-facing:** See rural-health needs, health-equity hotspots, and citizen outcomes clearly enough to authorize an actionable first move without absorbing unnecessary clinical-data liability. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
- **SHPDA / program leadership:** Move RHTP funds, provider readiness, and compliance reporting with clear milestone and outcome visibility. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
- **Med-QUEST / HAP:** Establish reliable claims/APCD governance, data definitions, security, and cross-vendor execution. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
- **Providers and CBOs:** Receive practical support, funding, coordination, and usable data without adding heavy administrative burden. [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]
- **Internal account team:** Select one bounded story and identify the right accountable stakeholders before attempting deeper architecture or solution design. [[inputs/interviews/2026-08-10-team-sync]]
