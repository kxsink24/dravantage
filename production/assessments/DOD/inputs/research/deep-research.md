# Deep Research Synthesis — Hawaii State Department of Health (DOH)

> Bounded synthesis of the current connected source set: Slack, Org62, Google Workspace documents, and the architect-provided Gemini/public-record briefing. This is not a separate independent web-search run. The Gemini report is preserved in [[gemini-deep-research]].

## Executive briefing

The current evidence points to a Hawaii RHTP transformation with a large funding/procurement window, multiple public-sector and ecosystem owners, and a difficult choice between broad clinical-data ambition and a lower-friction first operational outcome. [[inputs/research/org62-account-snapshot]] [[inputs/research/slack-history]] [[inputs/customer-docs/2026-08-13-hawaii-rhtp-executive-briefing]]

## Account and opportunity signals

Org62 shows Hawaii State Department of Health (DOH) as a Honolulu, HI account owned by Krystel Posadas. The account has active RHTP/SHPDA opportunities covering clinical/claims EMPI, grants management, analytics, HIE/clinical-claims integration, and a SHPDA CTO opportunity. [[inputs/research/org62-account-snapshot]]

The selected DRA opportunity is the near-term RHTP/SHPDA/HHIE clinical and claims EMPI opportunity. The adjacent grants-management and HIE integration opportunities should remain in scope as account context, not as assumed DRA requirements. [[inputs/research/org62-account-snapshot]]

## Public-research signals

The Gemini/public-record briefing identifies RHTP initiatives, AHEAD readiness, grants/compliance, Virtual CIN/MSO/provider readiness, HHIE, APCD/IDAP, privacy, identity, data quality, and cross-vendor accountability as the main research themes. It ranks RHTP sub-grant lifecycle/compliance visibility and provider-network readiness as lower-friction first-use-case hypotheses. [[gemini-deep-research]]

## Customer language and positioning

The Slack channel repeatedly supports:

- outcome-first messaging,
- a path of least resistance,
- no implication that Salesforce replaces existing systems,
- a high-level/agnostic data-foundation story,
- explicit attention to governance, PII, and accountability,
- one bounded first use case rather than “we can do everything.” [[inputs/research/slack-history]]
