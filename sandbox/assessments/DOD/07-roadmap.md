## Roadmap Sequencing Principles

The Crawl/Walk/Run framework sequences investments to build on each other rather than betting everything on a big-bang transformation. The sequencing logic follows the Data Value Chain's golden thread:

1. **You can't Trust data you can't Unlock.** Crawl phase almost always starts with connectivity and integration.
2. **You can't Activate data you don't Trust.** Walk phase typically addresses MDM, governance, and data quality.
3. **Run phase is where AI and automation live.** Any AI initiative that arrives before the Unlock and Trust work is done is at risk of the GIGO failure mode.

The specific sequence for this customer should reflect their barrier severity profile — not a generic template. A customer with a High Time Trap but Low Blind Spot has a different starting point than one with the inverse.
