## Strategic Plan and Institutional Priorities

ODU operates under its five-year strategic plan, **"Forward-Focused: Where Innovation Meets Possibilities,"** approved by the Board of Visitors on **Dec 9, 2022**, effective Jan 2023. Built with ~300 stakeholders across 12 working groups → **30 goals, 103 strategies, 7 focal areas**: Academic Excellence; Branding/Marketing/Communication; Corporate/Community Partnerships & Economic Development; Philanthropic Giving & Alumni Engagement; Research Growth; Strategic Enrollment Growth; Student Engagement & Success. Backed by the **Monarch Investment Fund** — $25M ($5M/yr over five years). [1]

**Measurable targets by 2028:**

| Strategic Indicator | Target (by 2028) | Objective |
|---|---|---|
| Student Retention Rate | **82%** | Campus-wide undergrad success infrastructure [3] |
| Six-Year Graduation Rate | **57%** | Academic prep, engagement, advisory tracking [3] |
| Professional Licensure Pass Rate | 90% min | Programmatic excellence [3] |
| Athletic Academic Progress Rate | 930 min (4-yr APR) | Academic standards across teams [3] |
| Research Growth | **Carnegie R1** | National prominence; next higher R1 quadrant [4] |

**OMNI — "One Monarch Nation Innovation":** the central digital-transformation pillar. Designates ODU a **"Salesforce First" institution** — a mandate to prioritize systems that integrate with Salesforce in all future procurements. Sponsored by the **President's Cabinet**. Governed by a cross-functional Oversight Group (monthly through June 2025, now quarterly), led by **Brian Easton** (Exec Dir, Digital Solutions & Innovation) and **Matt Newton** (AVP Business Optimization & CRM Architect, Education Cloud + Marketing Cloud). [6]

## Enrollment and Student Outcomes Trends

2025–26 total enrollment **23,469** (17,504 undergrad / 5,965 grad). A ~929-student decline over ten years. [7][8][9]

| Component | 2014/15-16 Baseline | 2025/25-26 | Trajectory |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total Enrollment | 24,934 | 23,469 | ↓ ~5.9% |
| On-Campus | 21,912 | 15,156 | ↓ 30.8% |
| Exclusively Online | 3,022 | 7,212–8,616 | ↑ 138–185% |
| Undergraduate | 20,101 | 17,504 | ↓ 12.9% |
| Graduate | 4,571 | 5,965 | ↑ 30.5% |

Growing reliance on distance ed (ODUGlobal offsets on-campus drops). Degree production fell 5,280 (2016-17) → 4,743 (2023-24) despite a 14% rise in VA high-school grads. **International enrollment dropped 11.6% (2024→2025)** — 88 fewer students, **~$2.44M tuition loss**, compounded by a **$10.25M federal research funding cut**. [10] Strategic-plan enrollment targets emphasize online growth, Latinx recruitment, student-athlete retention. [4]

## The EVMS / Health Sciences Merger

**July 1, 2024:** ODU finalized integration with Eastern Virginia Medical School → **Macon & Joan Brock Virginia Health Sciences at ODU** (largest, most complex academic health sciences center in the Commonwealth). MOU initiated Dec 17, 2021. [13]

Brings together: EVMS School of Medicine, Ellmer College of Health Sciences, Ellmer School of Nursing, EVMS School of Health Professions, Joint School of Public Health (w/ Norfolk State, approved Oct 2024), EVMS Medical Group. 56 academic programs; Board of Directors established by the General Assembly. [13][14]

**Funding:** $65M state (2024-26 biennium) + $300M Commonwealth over a decade + $350M Sentara Health over ten years. Transitioned ~**2,195 EVMS employees** (incl. 430 residents) to state employees; added **1,440+ students**. [13]

**IT/data implications (key for the DRA):** synchronized EVMS credentials into ODU's identity repo (single **"MIDAS ID"**); large-scale workstation/email migration; extended **core Banner systems** access (Student, Finance, Financial Aid, HR/Payroll, BDMS) via midas.odu.edu; **OMNI "Salesforce First" mandate requires aligning new health programs to central Salesforce Education Cloud + Marketing Cloud**. [16][6]

## Funding and State Mandates

Funding/accountability shaped by **SCHEV**. Biennial assessments (even years) under 2005 restructuring + the 2011 Virginia Higher Education Opportunity Act. [20]

**Dec 2025:** SCHEV adopted **"Developing Tomorrow's Talent: The Virginia Plan for Higher Education"** (through 2032) — outcomes-driven, centered on **"credentials of value."** Metrics: retention rates, post-grad employment/wage outcomes, time-to-degree, % graduates in high-growth fields. [22] ODU is a priority for need-based aid (high low-income population). [21] EVMS integration (HB1400) mandated a five-year integrated strategic/financial plan to the Governor + General Assembly by **Aug 31, 2023** as a prerequisite to release $10M planning + $30M integration funds. [23]

## Peer and Sector Benchmarking

ODU partnered with **Cloud for Good** to migrate ODUGlobal from legacy **TargetX** → Salesforce Education Cloud, Experience Cloud, Marketing Cloud; used "Talent for Good" to embed Salesforce analysts in Enrollment Management (National Student Clearinghouse imports, MCE campaigns). ## Synthesis: Compelling-Event Evidence

Public evidence of urgent need for **real-time, integrated, AI-ready data foundation bridging Banner SIS ↔ Salesforce CRM**:

- **"Salesforce First" OMNI vs. Banner reality:** OMNI commits ODU to Salesforce as the unified platform [6], but **Banner remains the authoritative system of record** [17]. Without real-time integration, records update via batch → data lag that blocks real-time student-facing interfaces.
- **EVMS integration scale/complexity:** +1,440 students, +2,195 employees [13]; manual workstation migration + MIDAS-mediated provisioning show a fragmented environment needing modernization. [16]
- **Enrollment/retention pressure:** on-campus undergrad down 30%+ since 2014, degree production declining [10]; hitting 82% retention / 57% grad by 2028 needs automated, real-time student-success interventions. [3][4]
- **SCHEV performance funding:** "Virginia Plan" (through 2032) ties funding to retention + time-to-degree [22] → real-time data needed to power predictive interventions. [21]
- **Academic modernization:** Payne's "Mobility Mindset" stackable/accelerated pathways need real-time progress tracking; batch loads can't support it. [24]
