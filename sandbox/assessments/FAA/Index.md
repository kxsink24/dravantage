## Key Findings (running summary)

> [!NOTE]
> `dra-analyze-input` updates this section after each analysis run. Do not edit manually.

**Three Barriers (High confidence — 5 interviews + 2 deep research documents):**
- Blind Spot: **High** — No unified borrower identity across 7A/disaster/microloan/SBIC programs; NOCMS isolated from lending systems; contracts data not integrated into UCP; 5-year GSA delay for veteran certifications; Lender Match disconnected from E-Tran. Externally corroborated: PIIA auditors explicitly state "lack of a unified, enterprise-wide database prevents identifying improper payments before disbursement"; COVID EIDL bypass left ~50% of $360B in loan files without verifiable tax documentation.
- Time Trap: **High** — 40-75 manual EIDL verification letters/week (National Ombudsman); monthly 1502 batch reporting; SBIC Web maintenance crisis ("100s of thousands/year to patch"); 8(a) annual review workbooks (RPA compensating for missing API integration); UCP rebuilt 4 times in 8 years. Externally corroborated: 8(a) mass suspension of 1,091 firms (25% of program) caused by inability to process documents before deadline; IRS §6103 physical form workflow creates constant backlog risk; disaster OIG audit found 12/28 files missing required damage photos.
- Trust Gap: **High** — PIIA non-compliant (KPMG May 2026); FISMA IAM at Level 2 (regression); Power BI/NOCMS case count discrepancies; disaster OIG found only 1/28 files had required repair estimates; FPDS-NG in constant flux; NAICS too broad; UEI/EIN both needed for matching.

**Scores (High confidence — 5 interviews + 2 deep research documents):**
- Integration Health: **33 / 100** (avg of Connect 1 + Integrate 1 + Understand 1) × 33
- AI Readiness: **37 / 100** (avg of all 9 capabilities) × 33

**Scorecard:** Connect 1 · Integrate 1 · Understand 1 · Unify 1 · **Validate 1** · Protect 1 · Decide 2 · Orchestrate 1 · Act 1
*(Validate revised from 2 → 1: PIIA non-compliance + OIG disaster audit failures override interview-based partial positive signals)*

**Narrative thread:** SBA is under a two-sided compliance and budget pressure: a 67% FY2027 budget cut requires automation to maintain service levels with a smaller workforce, while simultaneous PIIA non-compliance and FISMA regression (9/10 domains below baseline) mandate a unified data foundation. The good news: MuleSoft Government Cloud is already deployed (FedRAMP Moderate ATO) and the full Salesforce stack (GovCloud Plus, Data Cloud at FedRAMP High) is available. The foundational problem is identity and integration — 7A, disaster, SBIC, certifications, and Ombudsman all operate in silos with no cross-program borrower view. Straight-through processing (GCBD) proves API-driven automation is achievable; the IRS §6103 MoU renewal (October 2027) is the priority procurement window for digital consent capture. Roadmap sequence: Unlock (identity + API-led integration on the MuleSoft foundation), Trust (MDM/Unify + Validate controls + Protect/IAM to Level 4), Activate (fraud detection, instant decisions, proactive outreach).
