## THE PROMPT

I need comprehensive background research on **British Columbia Lottery Corporation (BCLC)** to prepare for a **Data Readiness Assessment (DRA)** engagement.

### What is a Data Readiness Assessment?

A DRA is a strategic consulting engagement delivered by Salesforce's Global Public Sector (GPS) team to assess how ready a government or public-sector organization is to leverage data for mission outcomes. It evaluates three dimensions:

1. **Unlock** — Can the organization access and integrate data from disparate systems?
2. **Trust** — Is the data accurate, governed, secure, and compliant?
3. **Activate** — Can the organization operationalize data insights to drive decisions and outcomes?

The output is a phased roadmap (Crawl/Walk/Run) that positions Salesforce Data Cloud, AI, and platform capabilities to close capability gaps. The assessment is human-centered: we interview stakeholders across business, IT, and data teams to understand both technical architecture and organizational dynamics.

### Why We're Researching BCLC

BCLC is preparing for a September 2026 QBR readout. They have multiple strategic initiatives converging:

- **Cullen Report compliance** — Regulatory mandate for a flawless 360-degree player view to support anti-money laundering (AML) and responsible gaming requirements
- **Platform modernization** — Two major go-lives in September 2026:
  - New gaming platform (Melco)
  - New lottery platform
- **Customer engagement transformation** — Launching Rewards & Incentives program and Future Anthem real-time promotions system
- **Data platform evolution** — Replacing legacy Infosphere MDM with Salesforce Data Cloud (Data 360), resuming paused Slalom CDP work for profile unification
- **Agentforce expansion** — Exploring AI agents for terminal diagnostics, high-value player concierge, and integration-layer automation

Our DRA must deliver a roadmap that aligns Salesforce recommendations to these initiatives before the September QBR.

### What IT/Data Information Is Important

Please research and synthesize findings across these dimensions:

#### 1. Organizational Context
- BCLC's mission, structure, and regulatory environment
- Role in British Columbia's gaming and lottery ecosystem
- Recent or ongoing digital transformation initiatives
- Key executives and organizational structure (especially data, IT, and compliance leadership)

#### 2. Cullen Report & Compliance Landscape
- What is the Cullen Report and what does it mandate for BCLC?
- What does "flawless 360-degree player view" mean in the context of AML and responsible gaming?
- What are BCLC's current compliance challenges or gaps?
- What data integration, governance, or identity resolution requirements does this create?

#### 3. Current Data & IT Architecture
- What systems and platforms does BCLC currently use? (CRM, marketing automation, player tracking, financial systems, gaming platforms, lottery platforms)
- What is Infosphere MDM, and why might BCLC be replacing it?
- What Salesforce products does BCLC already have in their environment?
- What integration challenges exist between their gaming, lottery, and CRM systems?
- What data silos or barriers to a unified player view currently exist?

#### 4. Platform Modernization Initiatives
- What is the Melco gaming platform, and what does its September go-live entail?
- What is changing with the new lottery platform?
- How do these platform changes affect BCLC's data architecture?
- What risks or dependencies exist between these go-lives and BCLC's data readiness?

#### 5. Customer Engagement & Personalization
- What is the Rewards & Incentives program BCLC is launching?
- What is Future Anthem, and how does it enable real-time promotions?
- What customer data is required to power personalized offers and responsible gaming interventions?
- What role does real-time data play in these initiatives?

#### 6. Data Cloud & Profile Unification Strategy
- What was Slalom's CDP work that was paused? Why was it paused?
- What does "profile unification" mean in BCLC's context (online players, retail players, casino visitors, lottery purchasers)?
- What identity resolution challenges exist (multiple player IDs, anonymous vs. registered play, cross-channel touchpoints)?
- How does Salesforce Data Cloud (Data 360) compare to Infosphere MDM for BCLC's use case?

#### 7. AI & Agentforce Opportunities
- What are "terminal diagnostics" in a gaming/lottery context?
- What would a "high-value player concierge" agent do, and why is it valuable?
- What integration-layer agents might BCLC need, and what problems do they solve?
- What responsible gaming use cases exist for AI (self-exclusion support, early intervention for problem gambling)?

#### 8. Industry Context & Peers
- What are best practices in the gaming/lottery industry for player data platforms?
- What peer organizations (other provincial or state lotteries, gaming operators) have successfully modernized their data architecture?
- What lessons from retail, hospitality, or financial services apply to BCLC's player data challenges?

#### 9. Risks & Constraints
- What privacy regulations apply to BCLC (PIPEDA, provincial privacy laws)?
- What technical debt or legacy system constraints might slow data integration?
- What organizational or cultural barriers exist to data-driven decision-making?
- What vendor lock-in or contractual constraints might affect platform choices?
